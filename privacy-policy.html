Privacy Policy — AI Ops Dashboard / Drive Ingestion Pipeline

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This application ("the app") is an internal operations tool built and used solely by Mike Sloskey for Miller Family Industries (MFI) and affiliated organizations. It is not a public product and is not offered to the general public.

What the app does

The app connects to a user's own Google Drive, using the drive.readonly scope, strictly to:

  • List the contents of specific, pre-designated Google Drive folders that the account owner controls.
  • Read (download) files from those folders in order to copy them into the account owner's own private Google Cloud Storage buckets, as part of an internal document-ingestion and litigation-evidence-management pipeline.

The app never writes to, modifies, or deletes anything in Google Drive. It only reads.

What we do with the data

Files and metadata read from Google Drive are copied into the account owner's own Google Cloud Storage buckets and, in some cases, indexed into the account owner's own private Supabase/Postgres databases, for internal search and recordkeeping purposes only. This data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. It is accessible only to the authorized internal user(s) of this tool.

Data retention and deletion

Copied data is retained for as long as the underlying business or legal matter it supports remains active. The account owner can request deletion of any copied data at any time by contacting the developer below.

Third parties

The only third-party infrastructure providers involved are Google Cloud Platform (storage) and Supabase (database hosting), both acting strictly as infrastructure for the account owner's own data — not as independent recipients of it.
